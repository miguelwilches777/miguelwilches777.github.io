# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no framework (user's choice: he wants to understand the code and reuse it for future sites). Video list lives in `videos.json`. Deployed on GitHub Pages.

## Users
Recruiters and studio/agency tutors in Paris looking for an *alternant* in video editing and post-production. Many open the link on their phone, often from LinkedIn or an email, with little time: they want to see the work fast and know how to contact him.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Miguel Moreno, video editor (*monteur vidéo / post-production*), student in BTS Montage et Post-production at école Aurlom, Paris. Goal: land an alternance in editing and post-production in Paris. Success = a recruiter watches at least one piece and writes him an email.

## Positioning
Self-taught editor who learned through social media: comfortable with vertical, rhythm-driven content for social networks, motion/logo animation for real small brands, and AI-assisted creation, on top of formal BTS training.

## Operating Context
Viewed mostly on mobile, sometimes desktop. Site language: French. Works are a mix of vertical (9:16) and horizontal videos; some play with sound and controls, short logo/motion pieces loop silently.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- All works presented together as one body of work ("mes travaux"), not split into categories.
- Videos with sound: player + controls. Short motion/logo loops: autoplay, muted, loop.
- Web-compressed copies only are published; originals stay untouched and out of the repo (GitHub file limit 100 MB).
- Adding a video = add file to `videos/`, compress, add one entry to `videos.json`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Miguel Moreno. Title: Monteur vidéo / Post-production.
- Binding visual brief from the user: dark, sober, cinematic; the videos are the protagonists and the interface must not distract from them. Subtle motion (Emil Kowalski's principles).
- Primary action: email (miguelwilches2712@gmail.com). Secondary: LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/miguel-moreno-477165416/).
